Sort order this layout supports: shank (zone A, fastest read) → type (B) → key size (C) → cut length (D). If your storage is organized by shank first — e.g. separate ¼″ and ½″ blocks — the shank bar alone tells you which block to go to.

Pictogram set

Side silhouettes, shank up, at native resolution (52 × 64 dots, shown 1:1 per dot). Hollow box = bearing. Spirals show flute direction plus arrows for chip direction.

Printing it from the Cube

Batch, directly (Cube Plus PT-P710BT, desktop Chrome/Edge): use Print directly to the Cube above. USB is the recommended route; Bluetooth is experimental and can need the printer re-paired between sessions.

  • The first label of a batch has ~24 mm of blank leader (the head sits that far from the cutter). With chaining on, later labels don't.
  • Labels are fitted to the length you set (34 mm by default): the middle text zone narrows so print + margins + the printer's own feed add up to it. Calibrate once with a measured label for accuracy.
  • Save list as PDF only helps on a Windows PC with Brother's printer driver. On a Mac the printer can't be added as a system printer.

One at a time from the phone app:

  • Save PNG on your phone (4× is the safest default — the app scales it down cleanly). In Brother P-touch Design&Print 2, start a 12 mm label, set length to 34 mm and margins to the smallest option, then add the image and stretch it to the full 9 mm print height.
  • Black-on-white or black-on-clear tape both work; the QR needs a light background, so skip white-on-black tape.
  • The Cube's minimum label length is 25 mm, so 34 mm is fine. The cutter wastes extra tape at the leading edge of the first label — printing several labels in one job (one per page in the app) saves tape.
  • Test-scan the first QR with your phone before batch printing. At 2 dots/module it scans well with most phone cameras held ~8–12 cm away; keep payloads short (≤ ~45 characters) to stay at v2 and get bigger margins.
